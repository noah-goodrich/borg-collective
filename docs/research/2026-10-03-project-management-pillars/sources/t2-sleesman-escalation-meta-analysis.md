## Key Findings

- Escalation of commitment is a robust, costly decision error: decision makers maintain commitment to losing courses of
action even after negative news.
- The pooled analysis covers 166 independent samples, so "keep going on a failing project" is a replicated pattern
rather than a single-experiment curiosity.
- Surprise result: the prominence of sunk costs was LOWER than expected as a driver; personal responsibility, project-
completion proximity and other determinants matter too, so "just ignore sunk costs" is an incomplete kill rule.
- Sharing decision authority may lead to GREATER escalation, and opportunity-cost salience de-escalates in some
situations but escalates in others.
- Authors flag that most evidence is cross-sectional lab work, which limits how confidently it can be applied to real
portfolios.
