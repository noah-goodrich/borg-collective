## Key Findings

- Accelerate uses causal words ('drives', 'affects', 'impacts') while the underlying design is survey-based and cross-sectional.
- Doubts that surveys alone give an adequate signal for objectively measuring delivery performance.
- Only a small subset of the book's claims appeared in two peer-reviewed Forsgren et al. papers, which the reviewer says does not add up to a literature.
- The reviewer still endorses CI and trunk-based development, but 'not because of this research': the practices may be right while the evidence is weak.
