## Key Findings

- Little's Law relates average WIP, throughput and cycle time but only holds if five assumptions are met.
- The five assumptions: arrival rate = departure rate; all items that enter must exit; WIP neither growing nor shrinking; average age of WIP stable; consistent units.
- When flow data violates Little's Law the problem is the process (abandoned items, growing queues), which makes the law a diagnostic.
- Reduces blanket claims like 'cut WIP and cycle time falls linearly' to conditional statements.
