## Key Findings

- PRINCE2 Version 7 (Sept 2023; custodian PeopleCert) keeps seven principles (continued business justification, learn
  from experience, define roles, manage by stages, manage by exception, focus on products, tailor to the project) and
  seven processes.
- The seven 'themes' were reframed as seven 'practices': business case, organizing, plans, quality, risk, issues,
  progress. It adds a dedicated 'people' element and sustainability as a seventh performance target, and gives more
  weight to digital and data.
- Version 6's six performance targets (time, cost, quality, scope, benefits, risk) were the heart of its control
  model; V7 adds sustainability, echoing PMBOK 8's move to widen 'value' beyond the triple constraint.
- Structure is three-axis (principles = why, practices = what, processes = when), with governance (project board,
  management by exception, stage gates) as the distinctive element versus agile frameworks.
- The official method text is paywalled; this vendor is the only accessible description (see paywalled candidates).
