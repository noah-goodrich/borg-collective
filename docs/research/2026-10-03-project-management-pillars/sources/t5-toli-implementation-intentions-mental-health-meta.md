## Key Findings

- Forming if-then plans had a large effect on goal attainment in people with mental-health diagnoses or above-cutoff scores.
- The effect held across different mental-health problems and goals and across methodological approaches.
- Pooled estimate excludes one very large outlier; a large effect (d+ about 1) in experiments is likely optimistic
  in field use; lab goal tasks are short.
- Authors suggest integrating if-then planning into existing treatment, i.e. a supplement, not a replacement.
