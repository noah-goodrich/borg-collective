## Key Findings

- Developers allowed to use AI took 19% longer to complete issues in this RCT.
- Developers had forecast a 24% speedup and, after the slowdown, still believed AI had sped them up by 20%: self-reported speed is unreliable for AI work.
- The authors disclaim representativeness (16 developers, mature repos they knew well).
- METR now marks the result out of date and points to early-2026 follow-up data, so use as a measurement-validity caution, not a current effect size.
