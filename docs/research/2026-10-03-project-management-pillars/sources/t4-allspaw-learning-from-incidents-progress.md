## Key Findings

- Incident count is a poor proxy for learning: an absence of incidents is not evidence of learning, and presence of
  incidents is not evidence of its absence. This is a direct Goodhart warning for "fewer incidents" as a learning KPI.
- Learning cannot be controlled; organisations can only create conditions for people to learn, and different people take
  away different lessons.
- Frames the goal of analysis as the richest possible understanding for the broadest audience, and asks who is actually
  learning from post-incident activity.
- The source is a talk transcript from a consultancy; it offers a worked exemplar, not a trial.
