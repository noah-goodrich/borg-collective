## Key Findings

- Principle 8 states that sponsors, developers and users should be able to maintain a constant pace indefinitely.
- The principle names three parties, not just developers: sustainability is framed as a project-level property.
- The page offers no measurement guidance, so "sustainable" has to be operationalized from other sources.
