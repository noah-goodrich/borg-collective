## Key Findings

- A single staff engineer with four AI agents under a Spec-Driven Development workflow delivered an initiative scoped for a four-person squad in half the planned time.
- Reported 90% acceptance of AI-generated code on first review and an above-85% reduction in direct staffing cost (self-reported case outcomes).
- The authors conclude specification quality and institutional knowledge, not model capability, are the binding constraints on one-person squad success.
- The authors state there is little structured evidence on designing or evaluating one-person squads, so this is hypothesis-generating, not general proof.
