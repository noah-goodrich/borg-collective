## Key Findings

- After a year with a small team, the author reports no-backlog and cool-down are liked, and that separating shaping
  from building 'results in clearer goals'.
- Problems: sketches too abstract push shaping into the cycle; 'appetite' does not remove the need for estimation;
  betting during cool-down creates pressure; big/small batch assignments become preconceptions.
- He concludes 'following what another team does because it works for them does not necessarily mean it’ll work for
  yours': a fit/tailoring caveat that parallels PMBOK's and PRINCE2's tailoring principles.
- The author's team was also working split from the main team, which worsened communication of progress: context
  confounds the result.
