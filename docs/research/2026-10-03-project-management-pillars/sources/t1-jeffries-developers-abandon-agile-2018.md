## Key Findings

- A co-creator of XP and Agile Manifesto signatory argues 'Agile' has become 'big business' and that poorly applied
  'Agile' interferes with developers and raises pressure ('faux/dark agile'), while still benefiting enterprises
  through visibility.
- Most complaints arrive as 'Scrum sucks' because most organisations doing agile are doing Scrum, i.e. the framework
  label is being blamed for implementation drift.
- The practical lesson for a framework decomposition: the ceremony/role pillars (events, artifacts, certifications)
  can survive while the underlying intent (developer autonomy, learning) is lost.
- This is opinion with no data; useful as a falsification signal about how pillars degrade, not as proof.
