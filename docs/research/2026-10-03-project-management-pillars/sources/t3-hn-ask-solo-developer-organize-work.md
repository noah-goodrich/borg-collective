## Key Findings

- The poster says standard practices (issues, backlogs, kanbans) feel like overkill ceremony, yet velocity suffers from disorganisation and priority churn.
- Named failure modes of solo work: switching objectives, over-polishing, changing priorities.
- One respondent runs a one-person SaaS on a minimal three-column board (Backlog, In flight, Done) reviewed every 1-2 weeks, spending 10-15 minutes per week.
- Responses span text file to Asana: no consensus, no outcome data.
