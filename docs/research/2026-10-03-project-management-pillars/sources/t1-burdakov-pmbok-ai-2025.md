## Key Findings

- It argues the PMBOK Guide (7th ed.) has 'gaps' for AI software projects: limited focus on data management,
  insufficient support for iterative development, and no guidance on ethical and multidisciplinary challenges.
- Its stated conclusion is that PMBOK 'provides a robust foundation' but its principles 'often fail to address the
  unique complexities of AI projects', i.e. the generic decomposition under-serves exploratory, data-dependent work
  (the nearest analogue in this set to AI-agent orchestration).
- It recommends adding a data-lifecycle domain, iterative models, and ethics to project planning, rather than
  discarding the framework.
- It records that agile was still judged applicable by some respondents when applied to the lifecycle, so the critique
  is partial, not a rejection of agile.
- Evidence is a literature analysis and respondent ranking; it is a signal, not a test.
