## Key Findings

- Interrupted tasks were completed faster with no loss of measured quality: people compensate by working faster.
- The price of that compensation is more stress, higher frustration, more time pressure and more effort.
- The context of the interruption (same vs different topic) made no difference to the cost.
- Personality measures (openness to experience, need for personal structure) predicted individual disruption costs.
- Implication for measurement: time-to-complete alone would have hidden the cost; subjective load was needed.
