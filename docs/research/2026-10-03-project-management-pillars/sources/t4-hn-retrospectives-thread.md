## Key Findings

- Several commenters describe retros that become venting sessions with no resulting change.
- One commenter says retro remarks were later used against them in an annual evaluation — an example of the safety
  failure that blameless practice is meant to prevent.
- Another commenter thinks retros could be useful if tied to a lean waste checklist, i.e. structure would help.
- These are opinions and anecdotes; they illustrate the failure modes but prove nothing about prevalence.
