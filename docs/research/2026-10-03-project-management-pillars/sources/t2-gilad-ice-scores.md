## Key Findings

- ICE = Impact, Confidence, Ease; the Confidence term is an evidence-strength adjustment meant to discount opinion-based
impact estimates.
- The author explicitly says the scores are only a way to compare ideas, that they are not exact science, and that
sorting gives a hint rather than the best answer.
- Stated justification: people are bad at picking good ideas from bad ones, and decisions are often driven by opinions,
biases, egos and politics, so structured scoring shortens debates.
- Impact and Ease are admitted to be estimates; this gives a natural target for Goodhart-style gaming when scores become
the goal.
