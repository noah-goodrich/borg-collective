## Key Findings

- Kanban decomposes into just three practices (define/visualize workflow, actively manage items, improve the workflow)
  and four flow metrics (WIP, Throughput, Work Item Age, Cycle Time), the smallest framework of the set.
- Its stated strategy is 'optimize value by optimizing flow', balancing effectiveness, efficiency and predictability,
  so the unit of management is the work item and its age, not the project plan.
- It names risk and trade-off management as an outcome but gives no scope/cost/schedule structure, mirroring Scrum's
  omissions; WIP control, a Service Level Expectation and explicit flow policies live inside the 'Definition of
  Workflow'.
- The 2025 edition's own change list says it 'Deleted reference to immutability of Kanban' and made WIP control 'Less
  explicit (and hence more flexible)': the Kanban Guide moved toward tailoring while the Scrum Guide still insists
  Scrum exists only in its entirety (a framework disagreement).
- The Guide is versioned (2020.7, 2020.12, 2025.5), so even this minimal decomposition has shifted in five years.
