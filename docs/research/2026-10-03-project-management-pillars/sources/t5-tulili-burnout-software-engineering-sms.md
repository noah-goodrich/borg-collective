## Key Findings

- 92 relevant articles dating from the early 1990s were identified and mapped.
- Early burnout research in SE was mostly qualitative and has shifted to quantitative, data-driven work in the last decade.
- Machine-learning detection of burnout is described as a "de-facto standard" approach in recent work.
- The abstract does not list specific causes; the specific cause taxonomy (workload, role stress, etc.) was not
  verified because only the abstract was read.
