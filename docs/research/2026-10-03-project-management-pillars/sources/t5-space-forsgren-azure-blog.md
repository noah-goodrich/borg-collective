## Key Findings

- SPACE names five dimensions: Satisfaction, Performance, Activity, Communication, Efficiency.
- Metrics that only look at activity, speed or volume miss the capabilities needed for project success.
- Developer experience is described as central to personal productivity and also to well-being and satisfaction.
- The "multiple dimensions at once" design is itself the anti-gaming argument in the primary paper (not verified here).
