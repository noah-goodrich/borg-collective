## Key Findings

- The Definition of Done is the Increment's commitment: a formal description of the Increment's state when it meets the
  quality measures required for the product.
- It is a binary gate: work that does not meet it cannot be released or even shown at Sprint Review and returns to the
  backlog.
- It creates transparency through a shared understanding of what "complete" means.
- It is set by the team where no organisational standard exists, so a solo developer can author their own, but the Guide
  gives no evidence on how strict or lenient it should be.
