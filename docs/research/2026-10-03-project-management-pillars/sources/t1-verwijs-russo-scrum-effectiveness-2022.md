## Key Findings

- The model says Scrum team effectiveness depends on five high-level factors: responsiveness, stakeholder concern,
  continuous improvement, team autonomy and management support (thirteen lower-level factors).
- It was validated by SEM on data from 'about 5,000 developers and 2,000 Scrum teams', with 'a very good fit' (CFI =
  0.959, RMSEA = 0.038, SRMR = 0.035).
- Notably the empirically important factors are feedback loops (responsiveness, stakeholder concern) and improvement,
  i.e. the empirical-control pillars (inspect/adapt), more than roles or ceremonies per se.
- Qualitative evidence shows teams often skip the refinement discipline ('We lack the discipline to refine well'),
  another view of how a framework's parts degrade in practice.
- Caveat: the sample is a self-selected survey and the study does not compare Scrum with other frameworks.
