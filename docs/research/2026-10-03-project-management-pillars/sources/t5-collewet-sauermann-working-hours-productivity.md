## Key Findings

- Within-employee design: the same agent works different hours on different days because of central scheduling.
- Longer hours worked means longer average handling time per call, i.e. lower productivity.
- The effect appears even in a mostly part-time workforce, so fatigue is not just a full-time-overtime problem.
- Effect is on a speed metric; quality and long-run outcomes are not covered in the abstract.

## Verified Quote(s)

**Location reference:** Page 3 of the PDF (Abstract page).

> We find that as the number of hours worked increases, the average handling time for a call increases, meaning that agents become less productive. This result suggests that fatigue can play an important role, even in jobs with mostly part-time workers.
