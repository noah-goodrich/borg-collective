## Key Findings

- Time blindness and hyperfocus are described as defining features of the experience for developers with ADHD.
- Body doubling (co-working silently on a live camera) is mentioned as a technique "supposed to" address task initiation.
- A task that would take 15 to 30 minutes can sit on a to-do list for years; a medication appointment sat for three years.
- All claims are self-report; none are tested.
