## Key Findings

- Survey of 415 projects found adopting risk-management practices has a significant positive impact on project success.
- A dedicated risk manager also had a positive association with success; soft skills mattered.
- The authors flag non-probability sampling and perception-based questionnaires as the main limitations.
- It measures "risk management practices" in general, not risk registers specifically — it does not say a heavyweight
  register is the active ingredient.
