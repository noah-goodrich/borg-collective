## Key Findings

- High-AI-adoption teams completed 21% more tasks and merged 98% more pull requests.
- PR review time increased 91%, which the vendor reads as human approval becoming the bottleneck.
- Per the vendor, organisational DORA-style outcomes did not improve in step with individual gains (only partly shown on this page).
- Supports the idea that, with agents, throughput is limited by verification capacity, a WIP/flow constraint.
