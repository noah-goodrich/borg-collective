## Key Findings

- The DoD is a formal description of the state of the Increment when it meets the required quality measures.
- Work that does not meet the DoD cannot be released or even presented at review: it returns to the backlog.
- DoD creates transparency by providing a shared understanding of what 'done' means.
- No empirical evidence is offered in the Guide; the effect of DoD on outcomes is untested here.
