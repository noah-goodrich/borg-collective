## Key Findings

- Randomized, waitlist-controlled trial of an 11-session metacognitive telehealth intervention (n=46).
- Gains in self-rated work performance, executive functions and quality of life were maintained at 3-month follow-up.
- The authors themselves call for larger samples and additional objective measures.
- The intervention is clinician-delivered; it does not validate any particular productivity app or method.
