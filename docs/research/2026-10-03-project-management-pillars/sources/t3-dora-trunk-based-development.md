## Key Findings

- Defines trunk-based development as each developer dividing work into small batches and merging to trunk at least once a day.
- Contrasts it with feature-branch workflows that take days or weeks and involve multiple developers.
- Frames small batches as the enabling condition for CI and fast feedback; the 2024 report echoes this for AI-assisted work.
- For a solo operator the 'multiple developers' coordination rationale weakens, but the small-batch and short-lived-branch logic applies to agent worktrees.
