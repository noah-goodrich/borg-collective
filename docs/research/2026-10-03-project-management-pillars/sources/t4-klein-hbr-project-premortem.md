## Key Findings

- Premortem = assume the plan has already failed, then generate reasons; framed to make it safe for dissenters to
  speak up during planning.
- The article credits a 1989 study with the claim that prospective hindsight raises the ability to identify reasons for
  outcomes by 30%. This sentence was seen only in a truncated third-party copy
  (lmscontent.embanet.com USC course PDF), not on the live hbr.org page; treat the 30% figure as cited, not verified.
- No outcome evidence that premortems improve project success is provided in the accessible text.
- The live hbr.org page shows only the introduction and abstract; procedure and body are paywalled.
