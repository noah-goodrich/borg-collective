## Key Findings

- People with ADHD in SE struggle with task organization and estimation, attention to work, and relating to others; they
  also report physical and mental health issues.
- Reported strengths include creativity, puzzle solving and thinking ahead.
- Interviewees externalize via to-do lists and notes and use calendar apps and reminders.
- Pair programming is suggested as a tool for task initiation and fighting procrastination.
- Participants were self-selected, so prevalence of any strategy is unknowable from this study.
