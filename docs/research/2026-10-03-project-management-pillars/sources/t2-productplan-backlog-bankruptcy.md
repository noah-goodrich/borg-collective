## Key Findings

- Account of deleting every item not planned for a near-term sprint: over 600 items, with no reported repercussions and
a sense of relief.
- Core heuristic: "if an idea has high enough value for customers, it will come back", i.e., re-surfacing is the filter,
which trades possible loss of weak signals for lower overhead.
- Lesson drawn: a backlog is not a place for every future opportunity; a gate on what enters matters as much as
ordering.
- Author notes the approach does not suit organizations needing long-range roadmap certainty. Evidence is a single self-
reported case.
