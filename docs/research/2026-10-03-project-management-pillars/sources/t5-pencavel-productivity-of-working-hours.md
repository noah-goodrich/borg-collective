## Key Findings

- Output is proportional to hours below a threshold and rises at a decreasing rate above it (abstract).
- Data are munition workers, most of them women (abstract) — a physical-labor setting from a century ago.
- Findings are linked to research on long hours and accidents/injuries (abstract).
- Popular summaries cite a roughly 49-hour threshold; this card did not verify that figure in the body text, so treat
  it as secondary until checked in the PDF.
- Caveat: the popular "70 hours equals 55" claim is a secondary-source gloss, not in the abstract.

## Verified Quote(s)

**Location reference:** Page 3 of the PDF (the Abstract page), paragraph under "ABSTRACT".

> Observations on munition workers, most of them women, are organized to examine the relationship between their output and their working hours. The relationship is nonlinear: below an hours threshold, output is proportional to hours; above a threshold, output rises at a decreasing rate as hours increase.
