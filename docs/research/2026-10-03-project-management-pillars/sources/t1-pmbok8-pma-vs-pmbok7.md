## Key Findings

- PMBOK 8 (Nov 2025) cuts principles from 12 to 6 and performance domains from 8 to 7, and reintroduces about 40
  non-prescriptive processes organised in five 'Focus Areas' (Initiating, Planning, Executing, Monitoring and
  Controlling, Closing), reviving the Process Groups that PMBOK 7 had removed.
- The six PMBOK 8 principles are: adopt a holistic view; focus on value; embed quality; be an accountable leader;
  integrate sustainability; build an empowered culture. The seven domains are Governance, Scope, Schedule, Finance,
  Stakeholders, Resources, Risk.
- The author reports that practitioners found PMBOK 7's principles and domains 'too conceptual and disconnected from
  day-to-day responsibilities' and that the new domains 'strongly resemble the familiar knowledge-area categories':
  the process -> principles -> processes oscillation is itself evidence that no single decomposition is settled.
- PMBOK 8 widens 'value' beyond scope, schedule and cost to include quality, stakeholder satisfaction, benefits and
  sustainability, an explicit move away from the triple-constraint definition of success.
- The 7->8 change was driven by PMI research (the page cites 'over forty thousand data points' collected in 2023); the
  underlying PMI primary text was not fetchable and the claim rests on this vendor.
