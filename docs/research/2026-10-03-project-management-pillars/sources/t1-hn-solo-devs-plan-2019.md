## Key Findings

- Top solo-developer practice is lightweight: a version-controlled plain-text to-do or org-mode file with current
  tasks at top, or a small Trello/Kanban board; some report abandoning Trello as too much friction.
- A top-level reply says the main solo risk is not task tracking but 'building the wrong thing' because feedback
  was delayed, so 'social milestones' (showing work to someone by a date) matter more than the board.
- This mirrors Scrum's Sprint Review and Shape Up's shaping/pitch as feedback checkpoints, and suggests that, for one
  person, stakeholder-engagement and feedback loops are the load-bearing pillar.
- The thread is an unrepresentative, self-selected sample; do not treat counts as prevalence.
