## Key Findings

- Argues that McKinsey's framework mostly measures effort and output, not outcomes and impact.
- Contends measuring earlier in the effort-to-impact chain creates perverse incentives (the Goodhart mechanism applied to engineering).
- Asserts the McKinsey framework will do 'far more harm than good' to organisations and engineering culture.
- The reasoning is conceptual, not empirical (level 7); supports RQ3 qualitatively.
