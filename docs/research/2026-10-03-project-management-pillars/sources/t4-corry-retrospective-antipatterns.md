## Key Findings

- "Wheel of Fortune": teams jump from data to decisions without generating insight, so they treat symptoms and the
  problems return.
- "In the soup": teams spend retro time on things they cannot change, so the meeting feels fruitless.
- "Loudmouth": unbalanced participation wastes the collective-learning purpose.
- All three are process-design failures, not evidence that retrospectives cannot work; the article offers no outcome
  data.
