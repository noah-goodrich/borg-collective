## Key Findings

- Argues traditional registers fail because "risk" is treated as independent point events with multiplied
  likelihood and impact scores.
- Describes two lightweight alternatives used on real programmes: a living timeline diagram reviewed twice a week, and
  "futurespective" narratives (imagining good and bad futures).
- The narrative device is a cousin of Klein's premortem (fiction licenses controversial possibilities).
- Experience report only; no comparison of outcomes with and without a register.
