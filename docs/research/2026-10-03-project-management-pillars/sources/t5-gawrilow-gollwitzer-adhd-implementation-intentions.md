## Key Findings

- Children with ADHD who added an if-then plan to a suppression goal improved inhibition to the level of children without ADHD.
- Combining implementation intentions with psychostimulant medication gave the best performance (Study 2): the plan
  complements rather than replaces treatment.
- The outcome is a short lab inhibition task, not sustained task initiation or planning.
- No adult or workplace outcome is reported in this paper.
