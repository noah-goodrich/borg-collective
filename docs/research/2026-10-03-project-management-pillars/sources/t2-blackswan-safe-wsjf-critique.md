## Key Findings

- Critique 1: summing Business Value and Time Criticality is economically unsound because Cost of Delay is zero if
either factor is zero.
- Critique 2: relative-only estimation hides absolute magnitudes, so queue costs stay invisible and trade-offs cannot be
made.
- Critique 3: relative scores are hard to recall past roughly 20 items and vary by stakeholder, so output quality
depends on process discipline.
- The authors still endorse quantifying Cost of Delay, so the objection is to SAFe's relative-scoring implementation,
not to the underlying idea.
