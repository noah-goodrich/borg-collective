## Key Findings

- SDD (requirements, design doc, tasks, then agent) repeats Waterfall's heavy up-front documentation; the author says Big Design Up Front 'has proven to fail most of the time'.
- He estimates SDD makes you spend 80 percent of your time reading instead of thinking and calls that not worth it.
- His alternative is to treat coding agents as supercharging Agile: small increments with product backlog items built in real time.
- Matt's flow differs from the target of this critique in two ways: the spec is a short synthesis after a live interview, and the tickets are vertical slices; but it still writes a PRD before building, so the critique applies in part.
