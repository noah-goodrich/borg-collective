## Key Findings

- Top-level critique: 'productivity suite' skills are extremely specific to one developer's workflow and do not transfer; the useful skills are organisation- and project-specific 'tribal knowledge' that never leave a laptop.
- Another commenter says much of this could be one moderately sized AGENTS.md or CLAUDE.md, and that spelling out something like TDD may even hamper the model.
- A third prefers to build instructions incrementally from agent stumbles and finds imported presets unsettling because the agent takes cues from instructions you never wrote.
- Defenders note the exposure value ('you eventually recognise a nice opinion') and that the glossary example is explained by the linked file; one commenter says Matt is a nice guy with reasonable opinions.
- The thread is small (14 comments, 43 points) against 289k stars, so it is illustrative of scepticism, not representative.
