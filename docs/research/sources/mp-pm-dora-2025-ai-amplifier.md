## Key Findings

- The 2025 report's headline is that AI's primary role is as an amplifier of an organisation's existing strengths and weaknesses.
- The greatest AI returns come from the underlying organisational system, not the tools.
- A companion DORA AI Capabilities Model is published; the full report and data are linked from this page.
