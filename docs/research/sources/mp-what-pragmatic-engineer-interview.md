## Key Findings

- grill-me was inspired by Anthropic's Thariq Shihipar describing an agent interviewing its user; Matt turned that idea into a skill.
- Matt's 'leading words' idea: terms from classic books ('tracer bullet') measurably steer agents toward better code, so he now reads classic engineering books for more such words.
- He is moving his coding sessions to cloud agents (they keep running when the laptop closes and can be made 'multiplayer'), and describes a day-shift and night-shift split with fresh contexts to stay in the 'smart zone'.
- He has mixed feelings on TDD with agents: he increasingly asks agents to produce proof that code works, with or without TDD.
- Business context: AI Hero is his latest course business after Total TypeScript, described as having generated more than 2.5 million dollars in total sales.
