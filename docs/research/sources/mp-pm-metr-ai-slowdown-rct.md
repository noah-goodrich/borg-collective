## Key Findings

- In a randomized trial, allowing AI increased task completion time by 19 percent for experienced developers on mature projects.
- Developers had forecast a 24 percent speedup and afterwards believed AI had sped them up 20 percent, so self-reported speed is unreliable.
- No evidence in this study tests PRD-first or ticket-slicing workflows; no similar trial exists for Matt's skills (none found).
