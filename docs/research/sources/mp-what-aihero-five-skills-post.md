## Key Findings

- Matt's framing: agents are 'a fleet of middling to good engineers' with no memory, so you need 'extremely strict and well-defined processes'; skills encode that process.
- The flow he teaches is grill-me, then to-spec (formerly to-prd) which does not interview and just synthesises the conversation into a spec on the issue tracker, then to-tickets, then tdd; improve-codebase-architecture is the periodic design pass.
- grill-me is three sentences long, drawn from Brooks's 'design tree' idea in The Design of Design, and is positioned as the antidote to Claude Code producing a plan too early in plan mode.
- His stated principle: 'Skills don't have to be long to be impactful'; the right words at the right time matter more than length.
