## Key Findings

- Four phases: alignment by questioning, a written spec in the tracker, tickets sized to one context window, then implementation reviewed by a fresh subagent.
- The post concedes the full flow is too long for a two-line fix or a throwaway prototype and pays off only when a feature must survive several sessions or hand-offs.
- It gives no measured outcomes (defect rate, time saved); claims that fresh reviewers are more honest are asserted without data.
