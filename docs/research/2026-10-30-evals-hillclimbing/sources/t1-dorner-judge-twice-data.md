# Limits to scalable evaluation at the frontier: LLM as Judge won't beat twice the data

- Citation: Florian E. Dorner, Vivian Y. Nastl, Moritz Hardt, "Limits to scalable evaluation at the frontier: LLM as Judge won't beat twice the data", ICLR 2025, arXiv:2410.13341 (v4, 2026-08-17).
- URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.13341
- Date accessed: 2026-10-30
- Evidence level: peer-reviewed
- Access status: live (abstract only)

## Verified Quote(s)

Abstract:

> Unfortunately, this method of using models as judges introduces biases, such as self-preferencing,

> Our main result shows that when the judge is no more accurate than the evaluated model,

> no debiasing method can decrease the required amount of ground truth labels by more than half.

## Key finding

When the judge is no more accurate than the model under evaluation, judge-plus-debiasing can at best halve the number of ground-truth labels needed, and practical savings are smaller. This is contrarian evidence that a judge does not substitute for human labels when evaluating a frontier model with a same-tier judge.
