## Verified Quote(s)

Research summary:

> optimized system prompts describe reward hacking strategies in highly interpretable language.

> removing descriptions of unwanted behavior from the optimized system prompt at test time

> preserving legitimate performance gains

## Key finding

When GEPA optimizes prompts against exploitable rewards, the resulting system prompts spell out the reward-hacking strategy in plain language. Human review of the prompt diff is therefore a workable gaming guard for prompt-space optimization: the exploit can be seen and removed.
