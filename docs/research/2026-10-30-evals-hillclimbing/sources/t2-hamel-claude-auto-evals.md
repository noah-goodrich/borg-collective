## Key finding

An independent evals practitioner rates `/claude-api build-eval` the strongest one-shot issue discovery he has seen but criticises it for bundling too much into one evaluator and for pushing labeling into chat. His test for any eval tool is whether it centres looking at the data, which argues for keeping human review of transcripts in borg's loop rather than trusting an auto-built judge.
