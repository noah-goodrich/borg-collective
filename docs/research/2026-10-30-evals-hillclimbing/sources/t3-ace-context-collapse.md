## Key finding

Two opposite failure modes of iterative prompt rewriting: optimizers that prefer brevity drop domain detail, and repeated full rewrites erode content over time. The paper recommends structured incremental edits instead of whole rewrites. This is a warning that a "fewer tokens" objective can silently cut behavior the eval does not measure.
