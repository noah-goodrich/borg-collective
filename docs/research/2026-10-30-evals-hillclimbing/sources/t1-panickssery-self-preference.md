# LLM Evaluators Recognize and Favor Their Own Generations

- Citation: Arjun Panickssery, Samuel R. Bowman, Shi Feng, "LLM Evaluators Recognize and Favor Their Own Generations", arXiv:2404.13076 (v1, 2024-04-15).

## Verified Quote(s)

Abstract:

> One such bias is self-preference, where an LLM evaluator scores its own outputs higher than others'

> we discover a linear correlation between self-recognition capability and the strength of self-preference bias

## Key finding

LLM judges score their own generations higher, and the bias grows with the judge's ability to recognize its own outputs. This supports the rule that the judge model should differ from the model under test.
