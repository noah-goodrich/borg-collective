## Verified Quote(s)

Abstract:

> we identify 12 key potential biases and propose a new automated bias quantification framework-CALM

> significant biases persist in certain specific tasks

## Key finding

The CALM framework catalogues 12 judge biases (beyond position, verbosity and self-enhancement) and finds that even advanced judges retain significant task-specific biases. Judge bias is therefore something to measure per task, not assume away by picking a strong judge.
