## Verified Quote(s)

Abstract:

> prompt optimization is a search over the Pareto front of accuracy and prompt-token cost rather than for one prompt.

> often recovers only a narrow region of the front

> The accuracy-cost trade-off becomes a post-search choice, not a pre-search weight.

## Key finding

Accuracy-tuned prompts tend to grow longer, and folding accuracy and token cost into one weighted score ("scalarization") finds only a narrow part of the trade-off. CRAFT keeps a Pareto front, using separate accuracy-focused and cost-focused edit generators. I did not read its numeric results.
