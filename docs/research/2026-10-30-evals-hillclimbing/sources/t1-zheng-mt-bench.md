## Key finding

The founding LLM-as-judge paper names position, verbosity and self-enhancement as the core judge biases and reports that a GPT-4-class judge reached over 80% agreement with humans on open-ended chat preference, matching human-human agreement. That agreement figure is for subjective chat preference, not for objective correctness, which later work (JudgeBench) shows is much harder for judges.
