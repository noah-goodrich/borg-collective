Any consideration for a vision for the future of alarm management should be expanded to include related measures that aim to eliminate accidents altogether. The substantial human performance improvement needed to accomplish this vision cannot be accomplished by addressing alarm management in isolation. There are other closely related factors that impact operator performance. After all, the common problem of overwhelming alarm floods in industry today is not a root cause problem in itself.
