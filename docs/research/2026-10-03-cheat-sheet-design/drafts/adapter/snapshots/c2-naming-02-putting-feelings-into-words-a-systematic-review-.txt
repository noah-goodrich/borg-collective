An open-research package for a systematic review and random-effects meta-analysis of affect labeling — the psychological and neuroscientific phenomenon, originating in Lieberman et al. (2007), in which putting feelings into words attenuates emotional responses. Pre-registration status. A PROSPERO-compatible structured pre-registration is included in the repository (prereg/PROSPERO_preregistration.md) but was not formally submitted to the PROSPERO registry. Screening was conducted by a single reviewer (deviation from the pre-registered two-reviewer protocol; inter-rater κ therefore unavailable; documented in §4.6 of the manuscript and in data/screening/README.md). Headline result. Pooled Hedges' g = −0.49 (95% CI [−0.73, −0.26]) for peripheral physiology across 8 effect sizes from 6 studies (Kircanski 2012 contributes 3 of the 8). Lab-stratified analysis: g = −0.74 within the UCLA Lieberman/Craske axis, g = −0.23 in independent labs. Corrected in v1.1.0: three of the previously reported nine effect sizes were imputed nulls rather than extracted values; two have been extracted from the source tables and one (McRae 2010) dropped as unrecoverable. Egger's test crosses significance under the corrected set (p = .047). Contents. Full ~10,500-word manuscript, structured pre-registration document, PRISMA 2020 flow with structured per-stage counts, RoB 2 / ROBINS-I assessments, effect-size dataset, the derived screening corpus reconstructed from the canonical PubMed query, and Python code for byte-for-byte reproducibility of every numerical result and figure. The screening corpus is positive-unlabelled (14 confirmed includes; the remainder unknown rather than confirmed excludes), so it needs further annotation before use as labelled training data.
