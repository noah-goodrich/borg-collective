Transition of care is a vulnerable time point for patients. The EMS-to-ED handover is a high-risk information transfer interval where effective and complete communication between EMS and ED personnel occurs. Such miscommunication has the potential to cause delays in diagnosis, treatment, and serious events. Handoff tools, such as mnemonics, checklists, and templates can organize handoffs so that complete and accurate information is communicated. A clearly defined process of face-to-face communication of key patient information from prehospital providers to receiving ED providers is a critical quality measure that improves patient safety.
