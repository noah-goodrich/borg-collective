## Key Findings

- Handoff quality (adherence to I-PASS key elements) improved markedly and stayed improved for 18 months, and 86% of providers supported ongoing use.
- No statistically significant change in 30-day readmission, 90-day readmission, UTI, PE/DVT, surgical site infection or delirium.
- Authors offer: program may not have been comprehensive enough, analysis may be underpowered (1984 patients vs more than 10,000 in the NEJM study), confounded outcomes, and benefits may take longer than 6 months.
