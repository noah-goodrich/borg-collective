## Key Findings

- A 19-item checklist designed "to improve team communication and consistency of care" was followed by a drop in in-hospital
  death (1.5% to 0.8%) and complications (11.0% to 7.0%) across 8 diverse hospitals.
- The design is sequential before/after with no concurrent control, so it supports "associated with", not "caused by".
- The checklist is a communication/coordination aid (team pauses), not just a memory aid - the mechanism claimed is team
  behaviour, which later null results (Urbach 2014) put under pressure.
