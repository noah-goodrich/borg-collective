## Key Findings

- The original Danziger et al. 2011 drop in favorable parole rulings (about 65% to near zero within sessions) is equivalent to d = 1.96, more than twice the conventional "large" threshold.
- Meta-analytic depletion effects are d = -0.10 to 0.25 after bias correction, so the claimed magnitude is implausible as depletion alone.
- A simulation reproduces the pattern with a rational judge and a case-ordering artefact (favorable rulings take longer).
