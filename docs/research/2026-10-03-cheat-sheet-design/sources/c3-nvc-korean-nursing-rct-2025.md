## Key Findings

- Randomized controlled study: 8 hours of NVC education plus a 5-week daily reflection journal.
- Empathy and interpersonal relationship scores improved more in the experimental group than control.
- Stress and resilience showed no significant between-group change (stress even rose in the experimental group,
  possibly from journaling burden).
- Authors list limitations: single university, short period, self-report measures.
