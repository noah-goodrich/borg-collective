## Key Findings

- 12 Netflix users reported prolonged search time, heightened choice effort and moderate satisfaction.
- Users show high reliance and trust in recommendation lists, yet frequently feel frustration and disappointment when recommendations miss expectations.
- Authors conclude lists must balance effective guidance with room for user exploration.
- Design implication: a recommended-next-move will be trusted by default, so a wrong recommendation carries an emotional cost; show why it was chosen.
