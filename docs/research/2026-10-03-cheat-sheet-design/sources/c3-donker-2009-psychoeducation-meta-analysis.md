## Key Findings

- Pooled effect of passive psychoeducation on depression/distress at post-intervention: d = 0.20 (95% CI 0.01-0.40),
  NNT 9, from 4 RCTs.
- Passive = reading or listening, no clinician interaction; cheap and immediately deployable.
- Authors position it as a first-step intervention, not a treatment; the lower CI bound is near zero.
- Only 4 of 9,010 screened abstracts qualified: the passive-handout evidence base is thin.
