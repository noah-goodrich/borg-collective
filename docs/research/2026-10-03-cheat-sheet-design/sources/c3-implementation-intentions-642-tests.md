## Key Findings

- Across 642 tests the abstract-reported effect range is .27 <= d <= .66 for cognitive, affective and behavioural
  outcomes.
- Format matters: effects were larger for contingent (if-then) plans than other formats, when motivation was high, and
  when plans were rehearsed.
- Publication-bias correction shrinks the headline: trim-and-fill adjusted d = .35; Robust Bayesian model-averaged d =
  .15 with extreme evidence of publication bias and heterogeneity.
- ADHD samples appear among the cognitive-performance tests (Gawrilow et al. 2011a/b), but no ADHD-specific effect
  size is given.
- WHEN IT FAILS: no reliable effect on vaccination or voting; health-behaviour effects are small (.14-.32).
