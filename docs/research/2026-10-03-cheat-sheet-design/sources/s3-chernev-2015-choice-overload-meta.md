## Key Findings

- Four moderators reliably govern choice overload: choice set complexity, decision task difficulty, preference uncertainty, and a prominent effort-minimizing decision goal.
- When moderators are taken into account, the overall effect of assortment size on overload is significant, contrary to Scheibehenne et al. 2010.
- Satisfaction/confidence, regret, deferral and switching likelihood are equally good overload measures.
- Design implication: a list is most dangerous when the task is hard, the user is unsure of their preference, and they want to minimize effort - exactly a "what do I work on next" moment.
