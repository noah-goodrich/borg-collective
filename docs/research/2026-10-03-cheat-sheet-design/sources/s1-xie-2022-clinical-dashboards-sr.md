## Key Findings

- Across 11 RCTs, standalone clinical dashboards gave conflicting evidence on antibiotic prescribing and no effect on statin prescribing versus usual care.
- Effects appeared more often when dashboards were part of multicomponent interventions (opioid use for low back pain down, screening up, antibiotic prescribing for URTI down).
- Authors conclude there is only limited evidence that EMR-integrated dashboards used as feedback or decision-support tools improve medication use and test ordering.
- Implication: a status display alone is not shown to change decisions; it needs to be coupled to an action path (decision support, workflow).
