## Key Findings

- Participants who missed their weekly module within 2 days were randomized to receive an SMS reminder or not.
- SMS reminders had no effect on module completion, login rates, or practice of coping strategies.
- Reminders may speed time-to-login and increase time spent in modules: effects differ by measure.
- Authors point to tailored, motivational reminders as the untested next step.
