## Key Findings

- A 30-40 minute antisaccade task followed by Go-Nogo produced significant depletion effects across 14 samples (the abstract reports d = 0.31 to 0.35 with minimal heterogeneity; the numbers are not in a verbatim quote, see Location reference).
- Authors argue earlier nulls may reflect weak manipulations; a laboratory effect may exist only after intense self-control exertion.
- Design implication: even if depletion is real, it is shown after 30-40 minutes of intense lab exertion, not after ordinary decisions.
