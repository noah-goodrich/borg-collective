## Key Findings

- Users said the checklist could divert attention from the primary task (the patient) and altered workflow.
- Authors warn an excessive or badly designed list can cause "checklist fatigue" that blocks adoption of a better list.
- A visible champion (head physician) was "crucial"; scepticism from senior staff undermined use.
- The aid was used in situations it was not designed for - scope creep of the sheet.
