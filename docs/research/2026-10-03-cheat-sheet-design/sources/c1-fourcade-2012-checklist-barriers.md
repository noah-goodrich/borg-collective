## Key Findings

- Compliance (checklist used at all) averaged 90.2% but completion (items actually checked) averaged 61% - high "use" can
  coexist with low fidelity.
- Top barrier: duplication of items already in existing checklists (16 of 18 centres) - an aid that restates what is already
  tracked elsewhere is ignored.
- Other barriers: time spent "for no perceived benefit", ambiguity, lack of understanding of item timing, and hierarchy.
- Barriers were organisational and cultural and varied widely by centre, so the same sheet performed differently in context.
