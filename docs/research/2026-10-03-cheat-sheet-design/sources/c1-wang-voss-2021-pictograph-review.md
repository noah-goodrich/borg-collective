## Key Findings

- 48 of 56 included studies supported pictographs in patient education; six found no significant difference.
- Authors rate the evidence as "promising", not conclusive: populations, pictograph designs and home-made outcome measures
  differ too much to pool.
- Pictographs need to be developed and validated with the target audience and clinical experts - an untested icon set is not
  covered by the positive findings.
