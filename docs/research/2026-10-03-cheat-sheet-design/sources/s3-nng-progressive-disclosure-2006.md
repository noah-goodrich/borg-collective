## Key Findings

- Show only a few of the most important options initially; offer a larger set on request.
- Whatever users frequently need must be on the initial display; secondary display should be needed only rarely.
- Designs beyond two disclosure levels typically have low usability because users get lost between levels.
- Design implication: borg link needs the frequent decision on the first screen; cap layers at two.
