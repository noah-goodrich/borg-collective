## Key Findings

- Case comparison beat sequential, single-case and non-analogous presentation, as well as traditional instruction: d =
  0.50 (95% CI 0.44-0.56).
- Four reliable moderators: objective of the comparison, whether a principle is presented, content, and lag before
  testing.
- Supports showing two contrasting examples side by side rather than a single worked example or a list of rules.
- Evidence is from concept learning in classrooms and labs, not from therapy handouts.
