## Key Findings

- Headline: a model with marital satisfaction, thoughts of dissolution and affective interaction 'predicted divorce
  with 93% accuracy' (in-sample).
- The four negative codes (criticism, defensiveness, contempt, stonewalling) distinguished EARLY-divorcing from non-
  divorcing couples.
- Negative affect during conflict predicted early divorcing but NOT later divorcing; absence of positive affect
  predicted later divorcing - so the Horsemen are a partial, time-limited predictor, even in the authors' own data.
- Sample: 79 couples, 21 divorces over 14 years; small base for a multi-variable model.
