## Key Findings

- Study 1: children with ADHD who added an if-then plan to a suppression goal improved Go/NoGo inhibition to the level
  of non-ADHD children.
- Study 2: plan plus psychostimulant medication gave the highest suppression performance - the plan is additive, not a
  substitute.
- Outcome is a lab inhibition task; transfer to real-world task initiation or workflow adherence is not shown here.
