## Key Findings

- After mandated adoption in 101 Ontario hospitals, adjusted mortality went 0.71% to 0.65% (OR 0.91, 95% CI 0.80-1.03,
  P=0.13) and complications 3.86% to 3.82% (P=0.29): no significant change.
- The abstract frames earlier benefits as coming from "observational studies" and calls the effect of *mandatory* adoption
  "unclear" - the same artefact imposed top-down behaves differently from the artefact developed with the team.
- Compliance with the form was not equal to use of the form as intended (see Fourcade 2012 for the compliance/completion gap).
