## Key Findings

- After correcting for publication bias in Mertens et al.'s nudging meta-analysis, the authors find no evidence for nudge effectiveness overall.
- Evidence against an effect for "information" and "assistance" interventions; undecided for "structure" interventions (the category that holds defaults).
- Strong evidence of publication bias in all subdomains.
- Design implication: a default is plausible but its magnitude in the lab literature is probably inflated; do not assume d = 0.68 transfers.
