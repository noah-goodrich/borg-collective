## Key Findings

- Lower negative emotion differentiation (NED) is associated with more maladaptive regulation behaviors: pooled r =
  -.15 (17 studies).
- Effect did not differ between clinical (r=-.15) and nonclinical (r=-.16) samples.
- Association survived controlling for negative affect, but shrank to r = -.09 (n = 11).
- Small effect: granularity explains a few percent of variance in behavioral dysregulation; causal direction untested.
