## Key Findings

- No single agreed definition of executive functions after 100+ years; three core EFs: inhibition, set-shifting, working-memory updating.
- Defines working memory as holding information in mind while completing a task.
- Notes as many as 33 different components have been considered EFs by different authors; 'hot' EFs include motivation and emotionally driven decisions.
