## Key Findings

- Describes the feelings wheel as three tiers: primary, second-tier, third-tier, moving from the center outward.
- Claims naming feelings lessens overwhelm, impulsivity and intensity for ADHDers - asserted without evidence; compare
  Nook 2021.
- Notes 'different models of the Feelings Wheel... same basic themes'.
- Recommends keeping the wheel visible (print, fridge, phone) and a 7-step check-in.
