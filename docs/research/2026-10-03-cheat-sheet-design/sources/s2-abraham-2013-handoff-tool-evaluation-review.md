## Key Findings

- Of 36 handoff-tool evaluations, 42% were quasi-experimental and 50% observational; 94% measured handoff-related outcomes with 70% using surveys and 53% user satisfaction.
- The authors conclude the quality and rigor of evaluations varied significantly, limiting their ability to inform standardization initiatives.
- Most tools were electronic (64%) and intra-departmental (95%), so evidence on cross-team handoffs is thinner.
