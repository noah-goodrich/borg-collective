## Key Findings

- Not all clinical reminders are acted upon; many are disregarded, a chronic issue called alert fatigue.
- Effectiveness of reminders was linked to their frequency, presentation and content accuracy.
- Suggested fixes: better design, personalized content, training; mitigation evidence remains limited.
- Nine included studies only: thin base.
