## Key Findings

- Across 82 studies, 45% of cases that tested the decision-fatigue hypothesis found significant decision-fatigue effects (diagnostic, test ordering, prescribing, therapy).
- Roughly half the field-study cases show no effect, so field evidence is mixed rather than null or confirmed.
- Decision fatigue is inconsistently defined and inadequately operationalised in the literature.
- Design implication: treat "decision fatigue" as a possible risk with mixed evidence, not an established mechanism to design around.
