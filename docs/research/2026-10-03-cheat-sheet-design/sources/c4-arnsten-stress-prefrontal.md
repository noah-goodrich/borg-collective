## Key Findings

- Even mild acute uncontrollable stress rapidly impairs prefrontal cognition, especially working memory.
- Prolonged stress produces structural change in prefrontal dendrites.
- Implication for tools: an aid must assume the user's working memory is degraded exactly when the aid is needed; it cannot demand recall or composition.
- Mostly animal and cellular evidence; human extrapolation is an inference.
