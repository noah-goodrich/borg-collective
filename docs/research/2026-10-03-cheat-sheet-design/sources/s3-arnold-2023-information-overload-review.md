## Key Findings

- Review identified 87 studies, field reports and conceptual papers on prevention and intervention for information overload.
- Many papers address behavioral prevention; there are also many proposals on structural work-design (ICT-level and teamwork-level).
- The strength of evidence for these interventions is mixed.
- Design implication: "reduce information amount" is an untested-at-scale structural intervention; borg should instrument what is read, not assume reduction helps.
