## Key Findings

- 67% of 45 developers rated their most recent frustration as severe.
- Open card sort of recalled experiences converged on 11 cause categories (e.g. mapping behavior to code, broken tools).
- Authors explicitly propose a common framework as the basis for interventions - the closest located precedent for a
  fixed vocabulary of 'stuck' causes.
- No test of whether SHOWING developers the vocabulary changes outcomes.
