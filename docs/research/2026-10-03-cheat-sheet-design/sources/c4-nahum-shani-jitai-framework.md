## Key Findings

- A JITAI has six elements: distal outcome, proximal outcomes, decision points, intervention options, tailoring variables, decision rules.
- Defines receptivity: transient ability and/or willingness to receive, process and use support; function of internal (mood) and contextual (location) factors.
- Providing support when the person is not receptive may not help and can harm engagement, adding to intervention fatigue.
- Decision-point frequency should match how fast the tailoring variable really changes, balanced against assessment burden.
