## Key Findings

- Randomised 79 RA patients to 9 months of IFS group vs an education control; 68 completed assessments.
- Post-treatment gains in overall pain and physical function (both p = 0.04); sustained at one year for joint pain,
  self-compassion, depressive symptoms.
- No sustained improvement in anxiety, self-efficacy, or (blinded) disease activity.
- Authors conclude only feasibility/acceptability and that efficacy trials are needed.
