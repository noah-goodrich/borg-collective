## Key Findings

- Participants rated opening statements to a conflict; I-language and communicating the other's perspective each
  reduced perceived hostility.
- The best-rated opener combined self- and other-perspective in I-language (e.g., 'I understand why you might feel
  that way, but I feel this way, so I think the situation is unfair').
- Cites Simmons, Gordon & Chambless (2005): more I-language and less you-language associated with better problem
  solving and marital satisfaction.
- Method is hypothetical scenarios, not live conflict; effect on real behaviour is unproven here.
