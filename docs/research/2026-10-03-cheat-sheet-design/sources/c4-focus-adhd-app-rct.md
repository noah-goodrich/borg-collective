## Key Findings

- 73 adults with ADHD randomized to TAU, App, or App + medication discount for 3 months.
- No significant difference in mean treatment adherence (medication possession ratio) between groups.
- App adoption and usability ratings were favorable (100% adoption with discount) despite no adherence effect: liking is not efficacy.
- Only the financial incentive moved a secondary measure (medication-intake registrations), early in the trial.
