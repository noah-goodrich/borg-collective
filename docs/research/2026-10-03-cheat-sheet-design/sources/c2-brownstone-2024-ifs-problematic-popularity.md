## Key Findings

- IFS has a 'strikingly small' evidence base for a 30-year-old model: three studies cited (depression, RA pain, PTSD,
  dissociation).
- Authors argue current expansion of IFS use has 'moved beyond its evidence base'; excluded populations (e.g.
  psychosis) and possible harms noted.
- Evidence for IFS tests the therapy package, not whether 'parts' language itself is the active ingredient.
- Popularity metric: 45,764 Psychology Today therapists mentioned IFS as of April 2024.
