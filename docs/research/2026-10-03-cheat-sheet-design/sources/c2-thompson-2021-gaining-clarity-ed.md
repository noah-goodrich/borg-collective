## Key Findings

- Concedes 'a compelling body of research' links low negative emotion differentiation to adverse outcomes.
- But says conceptual and methodological questions remain, incl. disconnect between how granularity is defined and how
  it is measured.
- Measurement varies across studies; the construct is state-vs-trait ambiguous.
- Implication: 'more granular = better' is a correlational trend, not a settled mechanism.
