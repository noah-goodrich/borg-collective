## Key Findings

- Naming emotions before reappraising left participants feeling WORSE than reappraising without naming (Study 1, N=80).
- Study 2 (N=60, within-subject) replicated and extended to mindful acceptance; unrelated to social desirability.
- Proposed mechanism: naming 'crystalizes' the affective experience and makes it harder to modify.
- Implication: naming is not a universal first step; sequencing and what follows naming matters.
