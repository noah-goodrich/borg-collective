## Key Findings

- Supporting both focused and contextual views can improve interaction over single-view software.
- But the best style depends on the task, and the current research fails to give clear guidelines.
- Costs: overview+detail demands mental effort to integrate views (Hornbaek et al. 2002 quoted: switching required mental effort and time).
- Reading comprehension was better with overview+detail than with fisheye (Hornbaek et al. 2002).
