## Key Findings

- Labeling is argued to be IMPLICIT regulation: it works without feeling like regulation and shows experiential,
  autonomic, neural, behavioral effects like reappraisal.
- Reported boundary: labeling reduced anger but not shame in one study (Kassam & Mendes 2013), tentatively attributed
  to self-referential emotions.
- Authors themselves say limitations and boundary conditions are not yet understood as clinical interest grows.
- No pooled effect size is offered; evidence is a tour of individual studies.
