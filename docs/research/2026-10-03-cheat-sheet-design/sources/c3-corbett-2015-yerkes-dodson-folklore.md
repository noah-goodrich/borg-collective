## Key Findings

- Argues the Yerkes-Dodson 'law' began as a speculative animal-conditioning experiment and was converted into a human
  law via a simple graph.
- Concludes the empirical evidence for the inverted-U 'has no basis in empirical fact'.
- Implication for design: a hump-shaped 'optimal arousal' diagram is a metaphor, not a measured curve - do not present
  it as quantitative.
