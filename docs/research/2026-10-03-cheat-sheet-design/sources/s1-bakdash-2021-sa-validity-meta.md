## Key Findings

- Across 678 effects from 77 papers and ten SA measures, SA correlates with task performance only weakly: mean r = 0.26, with a plausible individual-effect range of r = -0.15 to 0.60.
- There was high unexplained systematic variation among effects, so SA scores do not reliably predict whether an operator performs well.
- The authors conclude that interventions that improve SA (including display redesigns aimed at SA) may not produce meaningful performance improvements, and that major SA theories may need revision.
- Implication for a status display: measuring or claiming "better situational awareness" is not itself evidence of better decisions; outcome measures are needed.
