## Key Findings

- The intervention was a BUNDLE (mnemonic for oral and written handoffs, training, faculty observation program, sustainability campaign), so the effect cannot be attributed to the script alone.
- Medical errors fell 23% (24.5 vs 18.8 per 100 admissions) and preventable adverse events fell 30% (4.7 vs 3.3 per 100).
- Inclusion of all prespecified key elements in written and oral handoffs rose significantly (14 of 14 comparisons), i.e. the format changed what information was actually transmitted.
- Oral handoff duration did not change (2.4 vs 2.5 minutes per patient): a fixed format did not cost time.
- Error reductions were significant at only six of nine sites.
