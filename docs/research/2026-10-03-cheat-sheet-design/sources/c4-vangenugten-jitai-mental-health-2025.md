## Key Findings

- Only 5 self-identified mental-health JITAIs found (bulimia x2, depression, insomnia, prenatal stress), samples 5 to ~264.
- Essential elements such as adaptivity and receptivity were missing; decision rules and decision points lacked empirical support.
- Passive sensing and ML were hardly used; initial usability/feasibility/effectiveness appear positive but evidence is early-stage.
- Implication: 'just-in-time' timing for ADHD overload is a design hypothesis, not an evidence-backed practice.
