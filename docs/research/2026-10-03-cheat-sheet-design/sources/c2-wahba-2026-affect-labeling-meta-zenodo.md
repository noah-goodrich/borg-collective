## Key Findings

- v1.1.0: pooled Hedges g = -0.49 (95% CI -0.73 to -0.26) for peripheral physiology, 8 effect sizes from 6 studies.
- Lab-stratified: g = -0.74 within the UCLA Lieberman/Craske axis vs g = -0.23 in independent labs (v1.0 reported
  -0.13).
- Self-corrected: three previously reported effect sizes were imputed nulls; Egger test crosses significance (p = .047).
- Screening by a single reviewer; preregistration not formally submitted - treat as hypothesis-generating.
