## Key Findings

- Mean effect of error management training: d = 0.44 across 24 studies (N = 2,183).
- Larger for post-training transfer (d = 0.56) than within-training performance; largest for structurally different
  tasks (adaptive transfer d = 0.80).
- Both active exploration and explicit error encouragement were effective components.
- Implication: pairing a problem pattern with a worked correction is plausibly most useful when learners meet novel
  situations, not rote repetition.
