## Key Findings

- Independent replication: labeling reduced the effectiveness of reappraisal in both studies (N=226).
- Hypothesised delayed benefit of labeling did NOT appear; effects returned to baseline at 1-2 day follow-up.
- Abstract states the literature contains 'contradictory results' on whether labeling helps regulation.
- Boundary for design: a one-off naming act should not be expected to change later state by itself.
