## Key Findings

- Material should have one main message statement - "the one thing the audience must remember" - in 1-3 short sentences.
- Information-design rules are binary and checkable: use bulleted/numbered lists, and split any list over seven items.
- The scoring is zero/one per item, 20 items, converted to 100, with 90+ passing: a rubric a designer can run on a draft.
- The guide itself says the Index "cannot take the place of formative research or pretesting" with the audience.

> The Index contains 20 items, each with a numerical score of zero or one. The individual scores are converted to an overall score on a scale of 100. Although 100 is an ideal score, 90 or higher is passing.

> Make sure the material has one main message statement. The main message statement is the one thing the audience must remember. The statement may be 1–3 short sentences.

> Use lists to break up text in the body of the material and make information easier to scan and read. Lists with more than seven items must be broken into sub-lists.
