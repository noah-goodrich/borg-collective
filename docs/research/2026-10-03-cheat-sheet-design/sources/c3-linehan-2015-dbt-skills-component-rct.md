## Key Findings

- 99 women with BPD and high suicide risk; skills-only, individual-only and standard DBT arms.
- Conclusion: 'Interventions that include DBT skills training are more effective than DBT without skills training'.
- This is evidence for taught, practised skills in a supervised programme - it does not test whether a handout alone
  changes behaviour.
- Population is severe clinical BPD; transfer to a solo developer is by analogy only.
