## Key Findings

- Cognitive load theory rests on the premise that working-memory capacity is limited and overload hampers learning; this card
  confirms the premise is the theory's core claim.
- A prominent author in the field identifies "problematic conceptual, methodological and application-related issues" - i.e.
  CLT is a useful design heuristic, not a settled quantitative law.
- Practical reading: use CLT to justify removing clutter, not to claim a precise load budget per page.
