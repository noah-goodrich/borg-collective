## Key Findings

- Focus-group participants wanted the patient summary extended with anticipated disposition - a field specific to their work.
- Participants generally endorsed the order and content of the other elements.
- Themes across all qualitative sources converged on changes for brevity and clarity.
- Authors say outcome research is still needed; this is a usability, not an effectiveness, study.
