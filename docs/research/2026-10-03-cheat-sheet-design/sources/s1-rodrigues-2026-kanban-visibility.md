## Key Findings

- In a 13-person documentation team, perceived communication and collaboration were rated significantly higher after Kanban adoption (Wilcoxon signed-rank).
- Participants attributed the gain to work visibility, visual resources and less reliance on intermediaries (the manager had been the main communication hub).
- A standardised card structure helped team members read the status of each item quickly; visual cues replaced some ad-hoc status messages.
- Limitations stated by the authors: one organisational context and few participants.
