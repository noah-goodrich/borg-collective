## Key Findings

- Describes the Window of Tolerance as a MODEL of long-term effects of severe trauma, applicable to PTSD, dysthymia
  and chronic anxiety.
- Frames self-harm and substance use as attempts to regulate an autonomic nervous system readily pushed into extreme
  states.
- Mechanisms (ascending dopaminergic tracts, prefrontal control) are 'proposed', and the practical proposal is
  pharmacological - the review does not test the clinical diagram.
- Implication: the three-zone chart is a trauma-clinic heuristic; applying it to workflow overload is an analogy, not
  an evidenced use.
