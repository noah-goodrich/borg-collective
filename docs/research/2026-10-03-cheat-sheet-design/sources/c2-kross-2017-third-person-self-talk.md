## Key Findings

- Silent third-person self-talk reduced a neural marker of self-referential emotional reactivity (late positive
  potential) within the first second.
- fMRI Study 2: lower medial prefrontal cortex (self-referential) activation when reflecting on negative memories in
  third person.
- No increase in cognitive-control markers - described as a relatively effortless form of self-control.
- Mechanism: psychological distance, the plausible active ingredient behind externalization (problem-as-object).
