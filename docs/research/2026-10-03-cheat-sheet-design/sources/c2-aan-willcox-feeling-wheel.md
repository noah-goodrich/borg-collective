## Key Findings

- The Feeling Wheel is a 1982 Transactional Analysis Journal article by Gloria Willcox.
- Framed as a vocabulary scaffold that lets a person move from broad categories toward more specific words.
- The page states the wheel is not a diagnosis, measure, treatment or proof of a fixed emotion structure.
- The page itself makes no effectiveness claim - and I found no published effectiveness evaluation of the Willcox
  wheel (gap).
