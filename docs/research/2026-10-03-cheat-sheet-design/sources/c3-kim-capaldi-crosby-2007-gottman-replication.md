## Key Findings

- Abstract: 'The major findings of Gottman et al. failed to replicate' in 85 married/cohabiting at-risk couples
  assessed ~age 21 and followed ~2.5 years.
- Only 2 of the 22 affective processes were significantly associated with relationship status.
- Authors say the generalizability of Gottman's affective-process models is 'in question' and advise caution before
  clinician recommendations.
- Does not isolate the Four Horsemen by name; contempt appears within a high-intensity negative affect cluster.

## Inclusion Decision

**Decision:** Core
**Rationale:** The strongest fetched falsification evidence for 'Gottman prediction' claims; population differs (young
at-risk, short follow-up), which limits how far it overturns the original. Essential counterweight for any design that
cites Four Horsemen prediction.

**Redundancy check:** Complements Gottman & Levenson 2000; Heyman & Slep 2001 would add the cross-validation argument
but its full text was not accessible.

**Perspective category:** Contrarian
