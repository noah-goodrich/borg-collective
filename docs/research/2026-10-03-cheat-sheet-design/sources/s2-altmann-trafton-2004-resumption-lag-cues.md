## Key Findings

- Resumption lag after an interruption was double the normal inter-action interval (3.8 s vs 1.9 s) in this task environment.
- Cues present immediately BEFORE the interruption reduced resumption lag - the authors read this as people spontaneously preparing to resume.
- Implication for design: a re-entry surface that preserves or re-presents the last-seen context is doing what the cue-availability effect predicts.
