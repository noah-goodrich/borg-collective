## Key Findings

- Miller's "seven plus or minus two" was a rough rhetorical estimate; when chunking and rehearsal are controlled, capacity
  averages about four chunks.
- The limit applies to chunks, so recoding items into larger known units raises effective capacity - the logic behind
  grouping and familiar labels on an aid.
- The limit is observable only under stated conditions (e.g., rehearsal/long-term-memory recoding blocked); it is a limit on
  holding things in mind, not on reading from a page.
