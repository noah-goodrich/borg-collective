## Key Findings

- 23 laboratories (total N = 2,141) ran a standardized sequential-task ego-depletion protocol.
- Pooled effect d = 0.04, 95% CI [-0.07, 0.15], i.e. indistinguishable from zero.
- The abstract notes that earlier meta-analyses had found a medium effect and later ones questioned size and existence.
