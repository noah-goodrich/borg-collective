## Key Findings

- Emotional symptoms are common and persistent in youth and adults with ADHD.
- Emotional impulsivity and deficient emotional self-regulation may be specific enough to ADHD to serve as diagnostic criteria.
- Debate persists about whether these are core features; terminology is inconsistent.
