## Key Findings

- Study 3 (51 users, 5 site versions): concise +58% measured usability, scannable +47%, objective +27%, all three combined +124%, versus a promotional control.
- Qualitative: 8 participants said news, summaries and conclusions up front is useful and saves time (inverted-pyramid style).
- Users scan rather than read; short, non-promotional text is preferred.
- Design implication: put the conclusion first and cut words; but this is web text and BLUF itself was not isolated as a variable.
