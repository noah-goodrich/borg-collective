## Key Findings

- Labeling emotion in words, relative to other encoding tasks, diminished amygdala/limbic response to negative images.
- Labeling increased right ventrolateral PFC activity; RVLPFC and amygdala activity were inversely correlated,
  mediated by MPFC.
- Authors frame the result as a hypothesised pathway ('may diminish'), not proof of causal regulation.
- It is the origin point cited by every later review and critique (Torre & Lieberman 2018; Nook 2021; Ariely 2026).
