## Key Findings

- Narrative therapy 'views problems as separate from people' - the core externalization premise.
- It assumes people have skills, beliefs, values and commitments that reduce 'the influence of problems in their lives'.
- Frames person as expert in own life; questions asked from genuine curiosity.
- No effect-size evidence on this page; evidence for externalization per se is thin (Hu 2024 meta-analysis rejected -
  very low GRADE, not about externalization specifically).
