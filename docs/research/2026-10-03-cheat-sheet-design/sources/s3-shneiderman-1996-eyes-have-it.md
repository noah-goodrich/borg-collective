## Key Findings

- "Overview first, zoom and filter, then details-on-demand" is the Visual Information-Seeking Mantra.
- Seven tasks: overview, zoom, filter, details-on-demand, relate, history, extract.
- The author calls the mantra "only a starting point"; the paper is a taxonomy, not an experiment.
- Design implication: borg link is an overview; a per-project drill-in is details-on-demand; both are framed by a principle, not by a measured effect.
