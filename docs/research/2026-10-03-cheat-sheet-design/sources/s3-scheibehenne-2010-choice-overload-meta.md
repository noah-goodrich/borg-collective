## Key Findings

- Mean effect of assortment size on choice overload across 63 conditions is virtually zero, with considerable between-study variance.
- Several preconditions for overload were identified but no sufficient conditions, so "more options hurts" cannot be assumed as a general law.
- Design implication for borg: the premise "cut the list because choice overload" is context-dependent, not a settled mechanism; justify any cap by task difficulty, not by the generic effect.
