## Key Findings

- Barkley frames ADHD as a failure to USE known information at the moment of need, so interventions must act at the point of performance, not in weekly counseling.
- Internally represented information and motivation are 'extraordinarily weak' controllers of behavior in EF deficits, so information must be externalized (made physical) at the critical point.
- Motivation must also be externalized (artificial rewards/tokens) and sustained for long periods, like a prosthetic device.
- Claim is theory-derived; it is the design premise, not an effect-size result.
