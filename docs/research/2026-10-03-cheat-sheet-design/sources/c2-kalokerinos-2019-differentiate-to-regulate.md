## Key Findings

- Granularity did not predict WHICH regulation strategy people picked (unexpected null).
- Low differentiators got worse outcomes from the SAME strategies: all strategies (Study 1) and four of six (Study 2)
  were more strongly linked to increased negative emotion.
- Interpretation: granularity helps via effective use of strategies - supporting the 'precise label guides the right
  response' mechanism.
- Observational: cannot show that teaching words causes better regulation.
