## Key Findings

- Overall effect across Mayer's multimedia research is modest: g = 0.37, and it shrinks slightly each year.
- Largest effects: removing seductive detail, modality, personalization, the multimedia principle (words + pictures),
  sentence-level coherence, self-explanation. Cueing is only medium.
- Text + diagrams gives large, consistent effects on factual, inferential and transfer outcomes; animation/games/simulation
  are less consistent; virtual reality shows no significant effect.
- Implication for a one-page aid: cut decoration (coherence), pair text with a diagram, cue sparingly.
