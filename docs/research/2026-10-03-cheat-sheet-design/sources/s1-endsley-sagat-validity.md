## Key Findings

- SAGAT freezes a simulation and queries the operator on SA elements, comparing answers to the real state, which yields an "objective" element-by-element SA score.
- Query content comes from a goal-directed task analysis of the SA requirements for the domain, i.e. what the operator needs to know to do the job.
- Reported finding: the percentage of aircraft reported by experienced controllers fell as the number of aircraft in the simulation rose - evidence that perception of elements degrades with display load.
- The author claims high validity, sensitivity and reliability - a claim Bakdash 2021 qualifies for performance prediction.
