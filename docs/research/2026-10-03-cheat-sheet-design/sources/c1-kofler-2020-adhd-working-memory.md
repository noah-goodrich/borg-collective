## Key Findings

- ADHD is associated with very large central-executive working-memory deficits (d = 1.63-2.03; 75%-81% of cases impaired).
- The deficit covaries with inattentive and hyperactive/impulsive symptom severity across parent and teacher report.
- Phonological short-term memory (simple span) looks intact; visuospatial short-term memory has a smaller deficit that
  does not covary with symptoms - the problem is *manipulating* held information, not storing a little of it.
- Design implication (inference): aids should offload manipulation and holding (visible state, recognition) rather than
  rely on recall; adult generalisation is not shown here.
