## Key Findings

- The checklist concept "may encourage box-ticking without true fidelity" to the communication purpose it exists for.
- Regulator-mandated use has "on occasion" failed to improve outcomes (cites Urbach-type results).
- Thoughtful implementation with understanding of local context works, even in high-performing hospitals; the claimed
  mechanism is behaviour change (a culture of communication), not memory support.
