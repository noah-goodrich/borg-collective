## Key Findings

- Implementation relied on external teams giving longitudinal coaching over 18 months plus monthly metric reviews - format alone was not rolled out.
- Inclusion of all five key handoff data elements rose from 20% to 66% (verbal) and 10% to 74% (written).
- Handoffs with a high-quality contingency plan rose from 29% to 78% (verbal) and 24% to 73% (written); receiver synthesis rose from 31% to 83%.
- Reported handoff-related adverse events fell 47% (major events 1.7 to 0.9 per person-year); effect was similar for adult/pediatric and community/academic settings.
