## Key Findings

- Offers a quick-reference list of when/where/what/who/how prompts and tells the user to pick one or two rather than
  all.
- Warns that aiming for a perfect plan might backfire and recommends light-touch plans.
- Recommends designing for low-energy days: 'smallest possible version' and a 0-10 dread rating.
- Includes a post-hoc review ('did anything help at all, even a little?') - error-tolerant rather than pass/fail.
