## Key Findings

- 36 labs, N = 3,531, two depletion procedures: confirmatory d = 0.06, not significant.
- Bayesian meta-analysis: data about 4 times more likely under the null than under the informed alternative.
- Exploratory: effect was larger for people who reported more fatigue, but not moderated by trait self-control or willpower beliefs.
