## Key Findings

- A one-page chart with three zones (hyperarousal / window / hypoarousal) and symptom descriptors for each.
- Pairs each out-of-window zone with its own re-engagement actions (e.g., breathing and movement for hyperarousal;
  orienting to the present for hypoarousal).
- Teaches self-recognition first: 'become more aware of the symptoms that indicate you are outside of it'.
- Contains no outcome data; it shows the format in institutional use, not that the format works.
