## Key Findings

- A brief online emotion-word training did NOT directly improve negative emotion differentiation or distress vs control.
- Those who engaged more showed NED increases that predicted lower distress one week later (exploratory).
- Increases in emotional self-efficacy predicted lower distress at two months.
- Abstract: whether ED can be improved in adults 'remains largely unclear'.
