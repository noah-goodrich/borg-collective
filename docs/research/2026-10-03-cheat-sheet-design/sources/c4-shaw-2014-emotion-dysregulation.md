## Key Findings

- Emotion dysregulation is prevalent in ADHD across the lifespan and is a major contributor to impairment.
- May arise from deficits in orienting to, recognizing or allocating attention to emotional stimuli (striato-amygdalo-medial PFC network).
- Current ADHD treatments often ameliorate it too; field lacks consensus on whether it is core, correlated or a distinct entity.
