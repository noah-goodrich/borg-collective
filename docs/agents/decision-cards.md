# Decision cards and the decision gate

Why: on 2026-10-04, 2026-10-15 and 2026-11-25 a session needing decisions from Noah wrote terse lists ("Needs Noah: #97, close or restate scope") instead of asking through AskUserQuestion. Memory rules failed twice, so it is hardwired.

- **The gate (`hooks/borg-decision-gate.sh`, Stop, every session, plugin `HOOKS_JSON` and Cortex)**: blocks once (`{"decision":"block","reason":...}`) when the turn's final assistant message asks Noah to decide and the turn made no AskUserQuestion call. The reason tells the model to re-ask each decision as a card: what it is for a reader who has seen nothing, why it is open, 2 to 4 options with pros, cons and what each runs, the recommendation first, a source link.
- **Trigger, structural not a keyword**: a label line (`Needs Noah`, `Needs you`, `Waits on you`, `Decision needed`, `Decide`, `Your call`, `Open questions`, also after a full stop) unless its content is none; or two or more list items ending in `?`. Fenced code and block quotes are ignored. `stop_hook_active` always passes and every failure fails open. The turn is everything after the last real user prompt, so an AskUserQuestion in an earlier turn does not excuse this one.
- **Cortex**: Stop reaches the hook (`tests/fixtures/cortex/stop.json`, synthetic) and the transcript is read from `<id>.history.jsonl` beside `<id>.json`. NOT confirmed live that Cortex honors `decision: block`, or that `ask_user_question` is what the reason's AskUserQuestion maps to.
- **The checkpoint format**: `skills/borg-link-up` writes each `Waits on you` decision as a card (`references/decision-card.md`). `recon.core.checkpoint_owned_items` keeps the title as the row `text`, so pages and the brief stay one line and `borg_core/link/render.py` is unchanged; the whole card rides as `decision_card` (through `nextpick` rows and `borg link --json` `waiting.rows`). `borg next --json` already uses `card` for its chooser card, hence the name. A one-liner, even wrapped, parses as before: only indented LIST items make a card. `skills/borg-link` asks waiting cards through AskUserQuestion in rounds of 4.
- **Tests**: `tests/decision_gate.bats` (fixtures in `tests/fixtures/decision-gate/`), `borg_core/decisiongate/test_core.py`, `evals/decision-cards/` (offline trace pair, Tier 1 shadow of a Tier 2 grader).
