**Two gaps found by actually using what I built:**
1. **The schema could not express a dependency at all.** `gate.blocked_by` is prose by design, so a blocker that
   *is* a tracked item had nowhere to go — and `blocks` is the relation viz-3 needs. Added `gate.blocked_by_ref`.
   14 of the 72 historical edges are `blocks`, the least recoverable kind (`stacked` re-derives from topology).
2. **The backfill silently dropped the only cross-repo edge.** `blocks` does not merge components, so
   `ai-data-engineer#21` became a singleton and the two-or-more filter discarded it. Worse, my stats line reported
   the *source* cross-repo count as though it proved preservation. Now measured on output, with
   `kept + dropped == source` asserted.

**Backfill result, honestly:** 72 source edges → 13 programs, 53 rows; all 14 `blocks` and the 1 cross-repo edge
preserved; 430 items → 109 workstreams, 13 multi-item (the 9-PR dbt eval chain, the 5-PR team-token group).
**But only 1 of 72 edges crosses a repo boundary.** My earlier three-repo example came from the *other plugin's*
fixture, not borg's history. This recovers the ~40 `apex`/`blocks` edges nothing can derive — it does not conjure
the cross-repo graph the viz program wants.

**Verified `0 derived edges` on live recon is correct, not a regression** — every item carries `head_ref`/`base_ref`
