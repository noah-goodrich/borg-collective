**THE TRANSFERABLE LESSON, and it is one thing at four altitudes.** Every guard I fixed needed its own guard.
The two findings that justified three review rounds were both invariants ORPHANED BY A FIX: borg's model-floor
holding case, deleted when E4/E5 relocated and never carried over, and the relocated harness's outermost
`[ "$FAIL" -eq 0 ]` gate, which one of my own oracle cases CAPTURED as `rc`, printed in a success message, and never
asserted. Both were fully green under mutation before the fix. **The tell both times was a value printed and not
compared.** Grep new oracles for that shape.

**Second lesson, about the review method.** Majority-refute DISCARDS TRUE FINDINGS. Two real count errors died at
1/3 because skeptics are told to default to refuted when uncertain, and a skeptic that does not run the command is
always uncertain. Round 3 split verification — mechanically checkable claims (counts, paths, quoted output) go to ONE
agent that MUST run a command and report what it printed; judgment claims keep the panel — and reported zero findings
dismissed without measuring. Do that from round 1 next time.

**Third lesson, about prose.** Corrections generate corrections. Six confirmed count errors, and the worst was a fix
of mine that inserted `FIVE` on a wrapped continuation line and left `seven` above it, so the plan read
"The seven FIVE cases". A correction that leaves the word it exists to delete is worse than no correction. Measure
AFTER writing, not before, and re-read the wrapped line.
