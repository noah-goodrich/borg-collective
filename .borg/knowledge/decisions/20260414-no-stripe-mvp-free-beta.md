# 20260414-no-stripe-mvp-free-beta

## decision

No Stripe, pricing, or checkout in MVP. Free closed beta with pricing validated by user conversation instead.

## context

Setting scope boundaries for the MVP to stay within the ≤$25/mo infra cap and reduce build surface.

## reasoning

Pricing validation through conversation is lower cost and faster than building checkout infrastructure before knowing what users will pay for.
