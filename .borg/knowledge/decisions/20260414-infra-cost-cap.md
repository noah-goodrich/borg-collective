# 20260414-infra-cost-cap

## decision

Combined infra spend capped at ≤$25/mo pre-launch. No Stripe/pricing/checkout in MVP — free closed beta with pricing validated by user conversation.

## context

Portfolio directive needed a hard financial guardrail to prevent scope creep into paid infra before product-market fit.

## reasoning

Monetization infrastructure adds complexity and cost before there is any signal. User conversations are cheaper validation than building checkout flows.
