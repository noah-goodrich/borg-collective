# 20260630-vinculum-name-final

## decision

Name the cross-session bus 'vinculum', not 'link'

## context

Naming was in question during design; 'link' was a candidate

## reasoning

'link' collides with the existing `borg-link` command. 'vinculum' (Latin: bond/connection) is unique within the namespace and evokes the Borg Collective lore.
