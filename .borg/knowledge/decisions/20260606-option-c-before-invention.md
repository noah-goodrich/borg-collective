# 20260606-option-c-before-invention

## decision

Lead the next-gen roadmap with verification depth (Directive 01 fail-closed gate) before adding invention features (Contradiction Forge) or open distribution, in that explicit order.

## context

Council brainstorm evaluated 5 options across three axes: (a) invention depth, (b) verification depth, (c) open distribution. Option C (fail-closed gate) won; sequencing debate was whether to parallelize.

## reasoning

Distribution amplifies whatever ships. Invention on a self-certifying substrate manufactures pseudo-inventions at scale. Making verify real first means the invention and distribution layers are built on a substrate where integrity is enforceable, not assumed.
