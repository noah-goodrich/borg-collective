# obs-20260801-blind-review-research-before-incorporate

## content

Competitive research analysis (docs/research/2026-07-30-competitive-refresh/analysis.md) was blind-reviewed and came back REVISE before being incorporated into official docs. This caught issues that would otherwise have been laundered into authoritative documentation.

## resolution

Blind review of research/analysis docs should be a standard gate before any research artifact is referenced from or merged into primary docs. The REVISE→incorporate cycle added quality that a direct-commit flow would have skipped.
