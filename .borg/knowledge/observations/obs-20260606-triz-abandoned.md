# obs-20260606-triz-abandoned

## content

Meta-review finding: the TRIZ contradiction matrix was found to be near-random in controlled studies and was abandoned by Altshuller's own successors. Similarly, A-Lab's claimed '41 new materials' reduced to ~0 on re-analysis. These are canonical examples of invention-assistance tools that produce outputs indistinguishable from random on adversarial audit.

## resolution

Relevant to Directive 05 (Contradiction Forge): any contradiction-based invention feature must include a ground-truth check that distinguishes genuine contradictions from random pairings. The forge should not be shipped until the fail-closed gate (Directive 01) is in place, so contradiction outputs are subject to the same citation integrity requirements as research outputs.
